## Section 1: Partnership Origin & Decision-Making (3 minutes)

**Q1: How did the Charleston Trains partnership get started? What drew you to work on this problem? How did you begin the collaboration with Charleston?**

*Potential follow-up if needed:* "Was this a problem the City came to you with, or did you identify it and approach them?"

*Potential follow-up if needed:* "What convinced you that this was worth the effort and resources?"

## Section 2: Impact & Outcomes (3 minutes)

**Q4: What concrete outcomes or deliverables have you produced for the City so far? What does success look like for this partnership?**

*Potential follow-up if needed:* "Are there metrics you're using to measure impact? Has anything surprised you about the results?"

**Q5: Beyond the immediate research findings, what value has this partnership created for HAAG and your students?**

*Potential follow-ups if needed:*
- "Have students gotten opportunities they wouldn't have otherwise?"
- "Has this shaped how you think about applied research?"

**Q6: Has this partnership opened any unexpected doors—for funding, collaborations, or future projects?"**

## Section 3: Lessons Learned & Best Practices (3 minutes)

**Q7: What would you say were the key ingredients to making this partnership work? If you could distill it down to 2-3 critical success factors, what would they be?**

*Potential follow-up if needed:* "Was communication, regular check-ins, or flexibility particularly important?"

**Q8: What have you learned about working with external partners like municipalities that surprised you?"**

*Potential follow-ups if needed:*

**Q9: If you could go back and give yourself advice before starting this project, what would you say?**

*Potential follow-up if needed:* "How did you navigate those challenges?"
