Partnership Models

HAAG is a distributed research group exploring multiple topics and projects. Some of our work is best done independently. Some is strengthened through collaboration. We've developed flexible partnership models for when collaboration makes sense.

Our philosophy: We collaborate strategically and selectively. Not every research project becomes an external partnership, and that's by design. When we do partner, we commit to genuine mutual benefit and sustained engagement.

For Cities, Companies & Nonprofits

Some HAAG research projects are open to external collaboration. If you have a challenge that aligns with our work, we're interested in exploring partnership.

What We Look For

  • Research alignment: Does your challenge connect to one of our active research areas?
  • Mutual benefit: Can we both gain from this partnership?
  • Realistic scope: Can we define a specific, bounded problem with measurable outcomes?
  • Resource fit: Do we have capacity to engage meaningfully, given our distributed model?
  • Good partnership fundamentals: Clear communication, shared expectations, respect for constraints

Partnership Areas

HAAG research touches on these domains. If your challenge aligns with one, we'd like to hear about it:

  • Urban Computing: Transportation, emergency response, infrastructure optimization
  • Civic Data Science: Analytics, public participation, transparency
  • Smart Systems: Real-time monitoring, predictive models, adaptive management
  • Community-Centered Research: Work designed with communities for equitable outcomes

Getting Started

  1. Tell us about your challenge in 1-2 paragraphs
  2. Identify which research area might be relevant
  3. We'll have an exploratory conversation—no commitment needed
  4. Together we'll decide if partnership makes sense

For HAAG Researchers

If your research touches on any of the topics we explore, you might be interested in external collaboration. Our group maintains a directory of vetted organizations and potential partners.

Types of Collaboration

  • Advisory Relationships: Feedback and input from practitioners as you develop research

The Partnership Process

Here's how a typical HAAG partnership develops, from initial conversation to impact:

  1. Explore: Informal conversation to understand both your needs and our capabilities. No commitments yet.
  2. Scope: Define a specific, bounded problem with measurable outcomes. What does success look like?
  3. Propose: Draft a collaborative research plan with roles, timeline, budget, and expectations.
  4. Approve: Get institutional buy-in from both partners. This ensures sustainability and resource commitment.
  5. Execute: Launch the research with regular communication, iterative refinement, and course corrections as needed.
  6. Share: Publish findings, share learnings with the broader community, celebrate impact together.

Important note: Not every conversation becomes a full partnership—and that's okay. Some of the most valuable connections are one-off consultations, knowledge-sharing sessions, or introductions to other resources.
