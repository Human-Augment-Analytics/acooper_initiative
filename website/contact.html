Initiative Continuity & Handoff

This initiative was developed as part of an OMSCS initiative project and is designed to be handed off to ongoing HAAG leadership. The procedure, case study, and partner directory are fully documented and ready for a successor to continue without starting from scratch.

For HAAG Managers Interested in Taking Over

If you're a HAAG manager or faculty member interested in continuing, expanding, or taking ownership of this initiative, please reach out directly at acooper90@gatech.edu. A handoff would include:

  • A walkthrough of the full procedure and documentation
  • Introduction to the Charleston partner contacts
  • A review of active or potential partner relationships in the directory
  • Transfer of any ongoing communication threads

The goal is continuity, not ownership. If someone at HAAG is better positioned to carry this forward, that's the right outcome.

What Happens Next

1

Initial Response

We'll reply within 1-2 business days to acknowledge your message and schedule a brief call.

2

Exploratory Conversation

We discuss your challenge, goals, and timeline-no commitment, just an honest conversation about fit.

3

Next Steps

Together we decide what makes sense: deeper scoping, referrals to other resources, or a formal partnership proposal.
