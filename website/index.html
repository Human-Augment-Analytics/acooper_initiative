About HAAG

HAAG is a remote, asynchronous Computer Science research group within Georgia Tech's Online Master of Science in Computer Science (OMCS). We explore multiple research projects and topics—but we're intentional about expanding our impact through strategic, meaningful collaborations where they make sense.

Our researchers are distributed across time zones and geographies, working on problems in urban computing, data science, infrastructure, and civic technology. We're building a new model for how remote academic research can engage with real-world challenges.

Why We Collaborate

External partnerships strengthen our research. Real-world problems push our thinking beyond theory. And when we can help solve challenges that matter to cities, organizations, and communities, that's the whole point of research.

At the same time, not every research project is meant to be a partnership. We're selective. We pursue collaboration when there's genuine mutual benefit, clear alignment, and realistic capacity to deliver value.

Our Research

HAAG encompasses multiple research projects and areas of focus. Some are purely exploratory. Others are ripe for collaboration with external partners:

  • Urban Computing: Transportation systems, emergency response, city operations
  • Data Science & Analytics: Civic data, public participation, equity analysis
  • Infrastructure & Systems: Real-time monitoring, predictive systems, smart cities
  • Community-Centered Design: Research conducted with communities, not just about them
  • And more: Our group explores multiple topics—this is just the beginning

Not all HAAG research leads to external partnerships, and that's by design. We collaborate when it strengthens the research and benefits our partners.
