Get In Touch

Interested in learning more about the Real-World Collaboration Initiative? Have a research project that might benefit from external partnership? Or want to discuss collaboration with HAAG? We'd love to hear from you.

As a distributed initiative within HAAG, we work across time zones and prefer asynchronous communication when possible. But we're happy to meet via video call or email—whatever works for you.

What Happens After You Contact Us?

1

Initial Response

We'll get back to you within 1-2 business days to acknowledge your message and schedule a brief initial call.

2

Exploratory Conversation

We'll discuss your challenge, goals, and timeline. No pressure to commit—just an honest conversation about fit and possibilities.

3

Next Steps Determined Together

We'll jointly decide what comes next: deeper scope conversations, introduction to other resources, or moving toward a formal partnership proposal.

How We Communicate

The Real-World Collaboration Initiative is part of HAAG, a distributed research group. Our team works across different time zones and geographies. We've optimized for asynchronous communication and flexible collaboration.

Get In Touch

Email: rwc-initiative@gatech.edu
Response time: We typically respond within 1-2 business days
Preferred communication: Email and asynchronous messages work best
Video calls: We're happy to set up calls when needed—just let us know your time zone

Our Connection to Georgia Tech

The Real-World Collaboration Initiative is housed within HAAG, which is part of Georgia Tech's Online Master of Science in Computer Science program. This connection gives us access to institutional resources, rigorous research standards, and the Georgia Tech network.

If you prefer synchronous conversation, we can arrange video calls. Just let us know what works for you and we'll make it happen.
