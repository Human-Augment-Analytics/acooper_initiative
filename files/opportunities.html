Partnership Opportunities Directory

HAAG maintains a directory of organizations that are potential collaborators for our research. These are organizations we've identified as natural partners—either because they're actively seeking research support or because their work aligns well with topics we explore.

How to use this: If you're a HAAG researcher exploring external collaboration, browse this directory to identify potential partners. If you're an organization looking to work with us, you might see yourself here—or you might not be the right fit for current HAAG projects, and that's okay.

Next Steps

Interested in partnering with any of these organizations? Here's how to get started:

For HAAG Researchers

  1. Identify which organization(s) align with your research interests
  2. Note the contact information and focus area
  3. Contact the HAAG leadership to discuss outreach strategy
  4. We can help facilitate introductions and partnership development

For External Organizations

  1. Review our partnership models and process
  2. Identify the research areas or challenges HAAG could help address
  3. Reach out directly to the HAAG External Engagement Lead
  4. We'll discuss fit, scope, timeline, and next steps
