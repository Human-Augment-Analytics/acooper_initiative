## Question 3: Procedures Generated - What They Are & Purpose

### YOUR ANSWER (Ready to Use):

**What Procedures We Generated:**
The Real-World Collaboration Initiative has developed **one comprehensive procedure** that guides HAAG research projects through five phases of partnership development:

1. **Identifying Opportunities** – Determining when a research project has genuine partnership potential
2. **Scoping Partnerships** – Defining specific, bounded problems with clear success metrics
3. **Building Relationships** – Establishing institutional buy-in and formal agreements
4. **Executing the Partnership** – Managing active research with ongoing communication and iteration
5. **Measuring Impact** – Documenting outcomes and sharing learnings with the research community

**Purpose of the Procedure:**
To provide HAAG faculty and researchers with step-by-step guidance for moving from interesting research ideas to meaningful real-world partnerships. The procedure is grounded in the Charleston Trains case study and addresses the gap many academic researchers face: how do we systematically engage with external organizations in ways that strengthen both research and real-world outcomes?

**Governance Structure:**
- **Phase 1 Go/No-Go:** Faculty advisor + Initiative lead decide whether to proceed with scoping

- **Phase 3 Approval:** Georgia Tech OTT + HAAG leadership + External partner finalize agreements
- **Phase 4+ Oversight:** Initiative lead conducts quarterly reviews with faculty advisor

**Integration Points:**

- Semi-annual community reports share successes and lessons learned
- Partnership registry tracks all active and completed partnerships

- Procedure updated annually based on experience

**How partnerships are enforced:**
- Clear decision checkpoints at phases 1, 2, 3, and ongoing quarterly reviews
- Partnership registry maintained by initiative lead
- Quarterly reviews assess whether partnerships stay on track
